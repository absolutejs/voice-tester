## 0.0.4-beta.7 — 2026-09-28

### Added

- **Google Meet mode can speak and listen: say() plays Aura speech into the call through a microphone the tester controls, hear() waits for others to talk and transcribes it with timings, admit() lets people in when the tester hosts, userDataDir keeps a signed-in Chrome profile between runs, and a refused guest join now throws a clear error.** (`startGoogleMeet`, `StartGoogleMeetOptions`, `GoogleMeetSession`, `HeardSpeech`, `googleMeetChromeArgs`)

### Fixed

- **Joining Meet no longer crashes when the page reloads mid-join, answers the "Do you want people to see you?" camera prompt, and a kept profile that reopens its last tabs launches instead of failing to resize them.**

## 0.0.4-beta.6 — 2026-09-16

- **Expose an optional closed promise on transports; the Twilio WebSocket transport reports socket closure.** (`Transport`, `twilioWsTransport`)

### Fixed
